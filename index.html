whyBass is not heard. Bass is felt.

I got back into martial arts. I needed music that matched. Phonk worked — 808 kicks at 40Hz, dark pads, cowbell cutting through everything. But I didn’t know why it worked.

So I learned. The answer was physics. Sound is displaced air. Bass is force. The wavelength at 40Hz is 8 meters — longer than your body. Your ribcage is a resonant cavity. The kick doesn’t reach your ears first. It hits your chest.

That’s not poetry. That’s engineering. And it can be learned.
