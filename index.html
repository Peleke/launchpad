An 808 kick at 40Hz has a wavelength longer than your body. It doesn't reach your ears first. It hits your chest.

That's not a metaphor. Sound is displaced air. Bass is force. Rhythm is pattern. Harmony is the relationship between patterns. All of it is physics, and all of it can be built.

hljóð is an open curriculum that teaches music through strudel, a live coding instrument in your browser. You type code, press play, and hear what you wrote. You break it, fix it, and learn the name of the thing you just did. By the end you can take apart a jazz standard and put it back together differently.

How it works

You hear the thing. Then you change it. Then you learn its name. Then you use it to build something.

That's the whole method. Sound first. Jargon after. Every lesson ends with a track you coded. Share it or don't. It's yours.
